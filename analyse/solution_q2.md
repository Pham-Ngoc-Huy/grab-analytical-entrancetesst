# Q2. What is really driving this 45% growth?

The 45% GMV uplift is not the result of genuine behavioral change or organic cross-service adoption. It is materially driven by **incentive-driven transaction frequency and order splitting** as users actively exploit the 100,000 VND GrabFood qualification threshold.

While the top-line numbers show aggressive growth, transaction-level data reveals a mechanical response to the promotion rather than a natural shift in consumer demand:

* **The Qualification Spike:** The volume of GrabFood orders hitting the exact requirement (≥ 100K VND) to trigger the GrabMart voucher surged by **162%**.
* **Frequency over Value:** Average orders per user skyrocketed by **135.3%**, yet the total GMV per user grew by only 11.2%.
* **Basket Shrinkage:** Average Order Value (AOV) dropped by **9.2%** (from 130.2K to 118.2K VND).

Instead of purchasing more food per meal, users are deliberately splitting large orders (e.g., a 300K VND group meal) into multiple smaller transactions just above the 100K line to farm 50K VND GrabMart vouchers. The resulting GMV growth is built entirely on subsidized, artificial transaction volume rather than larger consumer baskets.

This incentive-driven behavior completely eroded unit economics, rendering the growth financially unsustainable. The sheer volume of subsidized Mart orders destroyed the overall Contribution Margin (CM):

| Segment | Incremental Impact (Promo vs. Pre-Promo) | Driver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GrabFood CM** | +149,348,100 VND | Increased transaction volume and delivery fees. |
| **GrabMart CM** | -655,422,800 VND | Heavy subsidy burden (50K voucher cost per order). |
| **Net CM Impact** | **-506,074,700 VND** | **Value-destroying growth.** |

The campaign successfully stimulated top-line transactions, but the incremental volume came at a severe margin cost. We are paying a heavy premium to teach existing users how to operate inefficiently on our platform, directly cannibalizing a previously profitable ecosystem to buy short-term, empty GMV.
